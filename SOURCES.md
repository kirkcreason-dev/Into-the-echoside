All downloaded FaceURL originals are PNG images despite local .jpg extension. Each is 7470×7350 pixels, 10 columns ×7 rows, with a 747×1050 pixel cell. Actual cards occupy top rows. Their lower right question-mark card is the TTS hidden-card placeholder, not game art. The -preview.png files are 2400 pixel wide reference previews. Card index is row-major, zero based. TTS card ID = sheet ID ×100 + cell index.

Local originals use FaceURL-<File ID>.jpg. card-art-map.json maps all 265 cells to name, category, sourceURL, filename, cell index, TTS ID, and crop coordinates. Names were read from scans because the TTS metadata contains no names or descriptions. Duplicate physical copies have separate entries.

Expansion cards display a gold three-interlocking-rings emblem at the lower right of the picture. The port mixes the base game, Oracle expansion, and promo cards into its decks; do not call the entire set expansion-only. The author says in a July 23, 2024 workshop comment that Gambits were not added. No Gambit scans or full rulebook are present here.
